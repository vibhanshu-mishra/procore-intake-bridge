# Privacy Review Template

This Phase I9 template is an offline maintainer and legal-review aid. It is not legal advice and
does not establish privacy or regulatory compliance. Complete the real review in an approved
private system with authorized counsel or privacy reviewers; keep only opaque references here.

## Placeholder sections

- purpose, scope, roles, and applicable obligations
- data categories, sources, uses, recipients, and access boundaries
- retention expectations and the difference between written policy and enforcement
- data-subject, consent, disclosure, and notification obligations requiring private review
- international, vendor, contractual, and customer-specific considerations
- open risks, decisions, owners, review dates, and private evidence references

Use placeholders such as `PRIVACY_REVIEW_REF_PLACEHOLDER`, `LEGAL_REVIEW_REF_PLACEHOLDER`, and
`PRIVATE_SECURITY_ACTION_PLACEHOLDER`. Do not add names, emails, customer data, legal notices,
assessment contents, or approval records.

I9 adds no consent management, data-subject-request workflow, breach-notification workflow,
retention enforcement, deletion/purge behavior, notification system, external call, or Procore
call. Private legal, privacy, security, and infrastructure review remains required before private
Sandbox, Pilot, hosted, or live use.
