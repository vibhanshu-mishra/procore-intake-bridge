# Pilot operations rehearsal

Use the G6 pack to rehearse the order of a private operator review without executing it:

1. Validate the placeholder profile offline.
2. Map G1–G5 plan references.
3. Confirm Sandbox smoke, read-validation, and linkage references are represented.
4. Confirm readiness, approval-packet, rollback, disable, monitoring, diagnostics, incident,
   support-bundle, and data-handling references are represented.
5. Resolve missing references and blockers in the private workspace.
6. Require a separate human review and keep launch on hold.

No linked report contents are read, and no live operation occurs. Dry-run success is not a
launch and is not pilot approval. Real identities, domains, URLs, infrastructure identifiers, logs,
evidence, secrets, and approval records stay private and manual.
