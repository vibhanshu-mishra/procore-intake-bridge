# Example private review backlog

This example contains opaque placeholders only. “Post-release” means after a future
human-approved `0.1.0` release. No release, build, publish, upload, tag, deploy, issue, ticket,
or approval is represented.

| ID | Review area | Private reference | Owner | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PR_SECURITY` | Identity and security | `PRIVATE_REVIEW_REF_PLACEHOLDER` | Maintainer | `NEEDS_REVIEW` |
| `PR_PRIVACY` | Privacy and legal | `PRIVATE_REVIEW_REF_PLACEHOLDER` | Maintainer | `NEEDS_REVIEW` |
| `PR_INFRA` | Infrastructure and operations | `PRIVATE_REVIEW_REF_PLACEHOLDER` | Maintainer | `NEEDS_REVIEW` |

Keep the underlying review system outside Git. A row is not an approval record.
